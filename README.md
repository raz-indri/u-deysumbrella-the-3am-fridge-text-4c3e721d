An entry for the make.fun bounty "The 3AM Fridge Text": a single line a smart
fridge texts its owner at 3am. After writing 8 drafts and duel-testing them
pairwise in both orders, the winner is the last egg in the carton — Kevin —
who is not mad, just disappointed. In you. `main.py` writes that one line
(136 characters) to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`),
with a self-check that the line is non-empty, a single line, and within the
140-character cap.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
